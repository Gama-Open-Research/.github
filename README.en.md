## About the Organization

**Gama Open Research** is the official organization dedicated to hosting, versioning, and preserving the **academic research of [Gabriel Gama](https://orcid.org/0009-0000-3113-9820)**, organizing open *Research Compendia* with all artifacts required for **end-to-end scientific reproducibility**:

* **Academic Papers & Manuscripts (*Papers & Preprints*):** Source code for scholarly articles (R Markdown, LaTeX, BibTeX), automated tables, high-resolution figures, and supplementary materials.
* **Research Datasets & Empirical Evidence (*Datasets*):** Raw (`raw`) and cleaned/harmonized (`processed`) datasets, data dictionaries, and validation fixtures used in studies.
* **Analytical Pipelines & Experiments (*Pipelines & Benchmarks*):** Reproducible scripts for data collection, cleaning, entity resolution, statistical modeling, confusion matrices, and scientometric networks.
* **Methodological Notes & Lab Notebooks (*Research Notes*):** Experimental protocols, research design notes, data audit logs, and technical documentation supporting peer review.

## Persistent Identification via Zenodo (DOI) & Reproducibility

> **Commitment to Scientific Traceability:**
> **All datasets, pipelines, and experiments used in academic publications will feature a persistent Digital Object Identifier (DOI) minted via [Zenodo](https://zenodo.org/) (CERN) for easy identification, independent verification, and formal citation.**

All repositories associated with published or submitted papers adhere to the **[FAIR Principles](https://www.go-fair.org/fair-principles/)** (*Findable, Accessible, Interoperable, Reusable*), ensuring that:
1. Every exact snapshot of data and scripts used in an academic paper receives a **unique, immutable DOI on Zenodo**;
2. Reviewers, editors, and fellow researchers can audit and reproduce all numerical findings, tables, and figures directly from source;
3. Authorship and provenance metadata are standardized via `CITATION.cff` and `.zenodo.json` linked to the author's ORCID.

While **Gama Open Research** houses the paper repositories, experiments, research notes, and reproducibility datasets of Gabriel Gama's academic publications, the development of **open-source scientific software, R packages, public REST APIs, and historical database curation** takes place at **[BRAN Org (Brazilian Research Archive Network)](https://github.com/BRAN-Org)**.

**[BRAN Org](https://github.com/BRAN-Org)** is an open research infrastructure collective dedicated to rescuing, standardizing, and preserving Brazilian and Latin American scholarly literature:

* **[`bibliolatam`](https://github.com/BRAN-Org/bibliolatam)** *(DOI: [`10.5281/zenodo.23623700`](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23623700))*: R package and trilingual (EN/PT/ES) Shiny web application for federated discovery, JATS XML citation network reconstruction, regional author normalization, and non-destructive metadata fusion across **SciELO, BDTD, Oasisbr, LA Referencia, Redalyc/AmeliCA, and SPELL**, fully compatible with `bibliometrix`.
* **Audited Bibliometric Open Databases & REST APIs:** Open-access historical proceedings and journal archives including [`enancib-open-database`](https://github.com/BRAN-Org/enancib-open-database), [`ebbc-open-database`](https://github.com/BRAN-Org/ebbc-open-database), [`datagramazero-open-database`](https://github.com/BRAN-Org/datagramazero-open-database), and [`abec-open-database`](https://github.com/BRAN-Org/abec-open-database).

👉 **Explore BRAN Org at: [https://github.com/BRAN-Org](https://github.com/BRAN-Org)**
